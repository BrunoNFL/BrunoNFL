## 🚀 My Journey

My journey into iOS development started unconventionally—not in the App Store, but deep within the iOS operating system itself. Driven by curiosity, I began by reverse-engineering iOS to solve real-world user problems, creating utilities that were celebrated by the community and featured on outlets like **iDownloadBlog**.

This background in low-level system modification gave me a fundamental understanding of iOS that I now apply to building modern, robust, and efficient applications with Swift. It's like having seen the blueprints of the house before becoming an architect.
